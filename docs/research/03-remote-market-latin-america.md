# 3. The remote job market from Latin America

Research from October 2026, written for someone in Latin America looking for remote or hybrid work with foreign companies. Same confidence labels as in [01](01-how-ats-work.md); some sources here are rated High, Medium or Low. This is **not legal or tax advice**.

---

## 3.1 How foreign companies hire people in Latin America

**Two routes.** As an **employee through an EOR** (an "employer of record" such as Deel or Remote hires you locally on the client's behalf; it costs the company roughly US$699 per person per month in Remote's Costa Rica example) or as an **independent contractor** (more common at small companies because it is cheaper).
Source: [Remote.com, Costa Rica](https://remote.com/country-explorer/costa-rica) — Medium.

**International hiring in Latin America grew strongly in 2025:** Chile +234 %, Mexico +53 %, Colombia +49 %. The most requested roles: customer service, data analysis and business development. "AI trainer" roles grew 283 % (US$15–75 per hour).
Sources: [Deel LatAm, Jul 2026](https://www.deel.com/es/blog/reporte-sobre-la-contratacion-en-latinoamerica) · [Deel Global Hiring Report 2026](https://www.deel.com/global-hiring-report-2026/) — Medium/High. Smaller countries get less of this demand than Mexico or Colombia ([Oyster](https://www.oysterhr.com/library/5-fastest-growing-remote-hiring-destinations-in-2025)).

**Time zones are an advantage.** Most of Latin America overlaps with US working hours. State your time zone and whether it changes during the year. Example: Costa Rica is on UTC−6 all year, with no daylight saving time, so it matches US Central time in winter and Mountain time in summer.
Source: [timeanddate](https://www.timeanddate.com/time/zone/costa-rica) — High.

**Why so many "remote" postings say "US only":** state-by-state tax and payroll registration, licensing in insurance and healthcare, privacy laws, working hours and pay-transparency laws.
Source: [FlexJobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/blog/post/10-reasons-at-home-jobs-require-locations/) — Medium.

**Healthcare has specific restrictions.** HIPAA does not ban access from abroad, but Medicaid cannot pay for services delivered by entities outside the US, Medicare Advantage requires disclosure of offshore subcontractors, and states such as Texas or Florida add their own limits.
Source: [Shumaker, Dec 2025](https://www.shumaker.com/insight/offshoring-patient-data-what-health-care-providers-need-to-know-now/) — High.
→ **Implication:** many US revenue cycle and claims roles (especially Medicaid work) will exclude Latin America for good reason. Look for postings that say "LatAm" or "nearshore".

**How to answer "Are you authorized to work in the US?":** truthfully, "No". If the form has room, add: "Based in [country] (UTC−X), available as an independent contractor or through your EOR." As a contractor you fill in form W-8BEN (not the W-9); if it is missing or wrong, the payer may withhold 30 %.
Source: [Gusto, Nov 2025](https://gusto.com/resources/articles/taxes/irs-form-w8ben-instructions) — High. The suggested wording is a recommendation, not an official rule.

**Typical pay in healthcare back office:** medical billing in Latin America pays about US$2,000 a month versus about US$4,000 in the US.
Source: [South](https://www.hireinsouth.com/roles/medical-billing-specialist) — Vendor.

**Basic voice and BPO work is shrinking.** Example: Costa Rica. In 2025, employment at multinational service centers fell for the first time in at least 8 years. The investment agency CINDE blamed the exchange rate, automation and bots replacing call centers, consolidation and competition from other countries. Higher-value work and work close to automation held up best.
Sources: [El Observador, Jan 2026](https://observador.cr/costa-rica-en-punto-de-inflexion-disminuye-por-primera-vez-la-cantidad-de-empleados-en-sector-servicios-de-trasnacionales/) · [CINDE](https://www.cinde.org/en/essential-news/cinde-attracted-19-new-investments-to-and-48-reinvestments-in-costa-rica-in-strategic-sectors-and-advanced-operations-in-2025-1) — High.

## 3.3 Tactics backed by evidence

- **Referrals:** 40 % reach an interview versus 3 % without one. Look for contacts at every good company. ([Ashby](https://www.ashbyhq.com/talent-trends-report/reports/referrals))
- **Apply early:** about 53 % of hires applied in the first week. ([Movemeon](https://www.movemeon.com/fr/insight/the-early-bird-gets-the-worm-apply-early-to-get-the-job) — Low/Medium · [BU](https://www.bu.edu/cds-faculty/2025/07/24/bu-careers-research-apply-early))
- **Remote has more competition:** on LinkedIn, remote roles were 8.4 % of postings but got 42 % of applications. Huntr: remote 3.5 % interview rate versus hybrid 6.1 %. ([Sherwood](https://www.sherwood.news/business/22-of-job-posts-on-linked-on-linkedin-are-hybrid-or-remote-work) · [Huntr](https://huntr.co/research/job-search-trends-q2-2026))
- **Do not over-apply to one company:** with 8 or more applications to the same company, the rate drops to 1.85 %. ([Huntr](https://huntr.co/research/job-search-trends-q2-2026))
- **Expect silence:** 52 % of US candidates were ghosted after an interview; about 19 % of postings were ghost jobs. Send one short follow-up. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2024/09/24/greenhouse-job-ghosting-hiring-rejection-email))

## 3.4 Remote job scams

Job scam losses in the US: more than US$220 million in the first half of 2024. FBI red flags: unsolicited offers by WhatsApp or text, tasks to "optimize" or "boost products", requests for payment (especially in crypto) to "unlock" work, and hiring with no interview or references.
Sources: [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/node/86951) · [FBI IC3](https://ic3.gov/PSA/2024/PSA240604) — High.

## 3.5 Working as an independent contractor (general overview, not advice)

Rules change from country to country. **Check your country's rules** with its tax authority and social security agency, or with an accountant. The questions are usually the same:

- **Tax registration:** whether you must register as self-employed, by when, and whether you must issue electronic invoices.
- **Income tax:** whether services you perform at home for foreign clients count as local income (in many countries they do).
- **VAT or sales tax on exported services:** whether it applies, and under what conditions it is exempt.
- **Social security:** whether contributions are mandatory for the self-employed, and at what rate.
- **Telework law:** many laws assume a local employer, so working for a foreign company with no local presence may not be expressly regulated.

**Example: Costa Rica.**
- You must register with the tax authority within 10 business days of starting. Under the general regime you issue electronic invoices and file VAT monthly and income tax yearly. ([Alegra](https://blog.alegra.com/costa-rica/como-inscribirse-en-hacienda/) — confirm at hacienda.go.cr)
- Services performed in Costa Rica for foreign clients are generally treated as Costa Rican-source income. VAT is exempt only if the service is consumed outside the country. ([El Financiero](https://www.elfinancierocr.com/economia-y-politica/que-es-la-territorialidad-en-materia-de-impuestos/6FE57XYHGFA2PALF7HNE2UBF6Q/story/) · [El Observador](https://observador.cr/el-iva-y-la-exportacion-de-servicios/))
- Social security (CCSS) registration is mandatory within 8 business days, and the self-employed contribute more than salaried workers. Someone with both a salary and independent income contributes on both. ([CCSS regulation](https://aissfa.ccss.sa.cr/arc/normativa/18/reglamento_trabajador_independiente.pdf) · [La Nación, Aug 2026](https://www.nacion.com/economia/debe-pagar-doble-cotizacion-a-la-ccss-si-es/4RIME24THZGERELM64C5DD7V54/story/))
- The 2019 telework law assumes a Costa Rican employer; working for a foreign company with no local presence is not expressly regulated. ([LexLatin, May 2026](https://lexlatin.com/entrevistas/teletrabajo-transfronterizo-costa-rica-ley))

## Missing or weak data

- Salary data by role and country is scarce in Latin America; Glassdoor and similar sites give rough ranges only.
- Most response-rate data (Huntr, Ashby, Gem) comes from the US market.
- Few sources explain how job boards verify that a "remote" role is open to your country. Always confirm it in the posting or with the recruiter.
