# Disclaimer

Please read this before using CV Factory. The onboarding asks you to accept it.

## 1. Only true information

CV Factory exists to help people present **real** experience well. It is built so that every sentence of a CV comes from information you confirmed, and it refuses to generate a CV with anything that is not traceable to your verified data.

Lies, invented experience, inflated numbers and misleading claims are not welcome. **If you provide false information about your skills, history or achievements, you alone are responsible for it and for its consequences.** The author of CV Factory accepts no responsibility for CVs built from false or misleading information.

## 2. AI usage and costs

CV Factory runs on an AI agent that **you** choose and pay for (a subscription or API tokens). Building your inventory, tailoring CVs and, above all, the daily finder (two runs a day, up to 10 or 20 vacancies per run, many sources) consume that usage. The more work you give the AI, the more it uses.

**You are solely responsible for your AI subscription, token usage and any charges.** The author is not responsible for any usage, cost or limit reached. The most capable model available is recommended for quality; it may also cost more.

## 3. No guarantees

CV Factory does not guarantee interviews, offers or any result. Job markets, applicant tracking systems and AI screening tools change. **Review every CV and every application before sending it.** You decide where to apply and what to submit.

## 4. Third-party sites and terms

CV Factory never applies for you, never creates accounts, never contacts recruiters, and never reads LinkedIn or any site that forbids automated access. You are responsible for following the terms of the sites and services you use, including job boards, Notion and your AI provider.

## 5. Your data

Your data stays on your computer (`my-data/`, `CVs/`) and in the accounts you connect (for example your own Notion). The AI provider you use processes what you share with it under its own terms. Do not put information in the project that you are not comfortable sharing with that provider.

## 6. Modifying the project

You may expand CV Factory (new sources, views, databases, documents). Changing the engine (`engine/`) is not recommended unless you know what you are doing: CVs may stop passing the tests. The setup warns when a core file has changed.
